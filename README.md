## Hi, I'm Sahar

I'm a data scientist based in London. I have an **MSc in Data Science (Distinction)** from Queen Mary University of London and a **BSc in Economics & Data Science (First-Class Honours)** from Royal Holloway.

I like problems where the modelling and the engineering both matter: building a model, testing it honestly, and then running it reliably. For my MSc dissertation I asked when a deployed credit-risk model should be recalibrated or replaced, when the loan outcomes that show it has degraded only arrive months later. I built and evaluated a policy to answer that, then ran it as a monthly job on Google Cloud.
